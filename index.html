Green Sighting

Measures horizontal angles only, clockwise from the reference you zero on. Hold the phone upright, stand directly over the station, and put the crosshair on each target.

Re-sight the reference every 5–6 captures and at the end. Each re-sight measures the gyro drift, and the angles are corrected for it.

Practice-green testing only. Needs camera and motion access.
